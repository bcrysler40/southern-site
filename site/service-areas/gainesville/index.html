Home Loans in Gainesville

Since 2006, Southern Home Loans has dedicated itself to providing excellent, affordable home loans to the people of Gainesville. We’ll quickly get you approved with affordable rates.

Because we live and work in Gainesville, we’re dedicated to serving the local community. If you’re looking for a good home loan, you should look no further than your own backyard. Ask us about all the options available to you.

We’re Committed to the Gainesville Community

We’re in touch with the Gainesville community, and we’ve been working with families employed by the University of Florida, helping them to get the home loans they need, for more than a decade. Gainesville was hit hard by the 2009 real estate crash but is well on its way to a full recovery, and we’re helping that progress by providing the best loan rates possible.

We’ve been successful at helping UF graduates get the homes of their dreams, and we’ve also helped many members of the retirement community get qualified for reverse mortgages to help them get by a little bit easier.

A history We’re Proud of

We were founded in 2006, and since then we’ve been the most respected name in home loans in the Gainesville area. Superior customer service is our commitment, and this is why we have the reputation we do.

Being a direct lender has allowed us to make the process of getting a home loan extremely easy. Within an hour, we’ll get you pre-approved for the loan that you need.

Graduates looking to either purchase or build their first home have counted on us, as have folks who want to refinance in order to save money on their monthly payments.

Reverse Mortgages in Gainesville

Getting a reverse mortgage is something that many folks 62 or over consider in order to cover expenses that they didn’t expect or to just enjoy retirement. But it’s important to know what you’re getting into before you agree to anything. Our experts are here to discuss all of your options and make sure that you understand the whole process. Ask us how you can use whatever equity you have in your home to get the money you need to take care of medical bills, supplement your income, or make improvements on your home without having to make payments until you no longer reside in it.

The Easiest way to get a Home Loan

Anyone who has ever tried to get a home loan before knows that it can take a lot of time and cause a lot of headaches. Thanks to our innovations in mobile and online services, we can get the process moving quickly.

All you have to do is choose the loan you want on the site to get all the information you need, fill out the quick form, and you’ll be on your way to getting approved. It’s never been easier!
