Your local mortgage lender in Ocala

Southern Home Loans is based right here in Ocala, at 2050 E Silver Springs Blvd. Our team has helped Marion County families buy, build and refinance homes since 2000, and we still meet borrowers face to face. When you have a question, you call a local loan officer who knows the neighborhoods, the builders and the title companies in town.

Whether you are buying your first home in southwest Ocala, moving into a 55+ community like On Top of the World, building on acreage east of town or buying near the World Equestrian Center, we will match you with the loan that fits and walk you through every step.

Home loans we offer Ocala buyers

  • FHA loans with a low down payment and flexible credit guidelines
  • VA loans for veterans and service members, with no monthly mortgage insurance
  • USDA loans for eligible areas around Ocala and across Marion County
  • Conventional fixed-rate and jumbo loans
  • Construction-to-permanent loans and lot and land loans
  • Marion County SHIP and Florida down payment assistance
  • Bank statement and DSCR loans for self-employed borrowers and investors
  • Reverse mortgages for homeowners 62 and older, or 55 and older with certain programs

Buying a new construction home in Ocala?

Marion County is one of the fastest-growing areas in Florida, and many buyers here are choosing new construction. Through our Builder Advantage Program, partner builders can offer qualified buyers a below-market forward-commitment rate on select new homes. See participating builders and homes.

Why Ocala buyers work with a local lender

A local lender knows Marion County appraisers, county assistance programs and well, septic and acreage properties that out-of-state lenders often struggle with. We can pre-approve you quickly so your offer is taken seriously, and our team is a phone call or a short drive away from pre-approval to closing day.
