Advantages of a VA Loan

If you’re a United States military Veteran, then it’s always a good idea to take advantage of a VA home loan. This is a loan that the United States Department of Veterans Affairs guarantees, and it’s provided by lenders who are qualified.

We want to save you money on your monthly payments and interest rates, and we’ve established a system that has proven to do just that. Southern Home Loans is fully qualified to offer VA Loans to military Veterans, and we make the entire process as easy as possible. We can provide long-term home financing to Veterans and their spouses.

Why and how do we make VA Mortgages simple?

At Southern Home Loans, we care deeply about those who have served our country. We understand the sacrifice that our veterans and their families have made, and we want to ensure that they never have any trouble securing their home loans.

We’ve been in this business for a long time, and we’ve set up the tools needed to make your application for your VA loan easy. Simply fill out the quick VA Loan Qualifier for free and be on your way to owning your home!

Frequently asked questions

Who is eligible for a VA loan?

Eligible veterans, active-duty service members, National Guard and Reserve members, and some surviving spouses can use a VA loan. We can request your Certificate of Eligibility (COE) for you.

Do VA loans require mortgage insurance?

No. VA loans have no monthly mortgage insurance. Most borrowers pay a one-time VA funding fee, which can be financed into the loan. Veterans who receive VA disability compensation are generally exempt.

Can I use a VA loan more than once?

Yes. VA entitlement can be restored after you sell and pay off a VA loan, and some veterans have enough remaining entitlement to buy again while keeping their current home.

Can I build a home with a VA loan?

Yes. We offer VA construction-to-permanent financing, so eligible veterans can finance the lot and the build in one loan with one closing.
