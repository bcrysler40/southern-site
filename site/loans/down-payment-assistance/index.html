Florida Hometown Heroes: up to $35,000 for first-time buyers

Hometown Heroes is Florida Housing's down payment and closing cost assistance program for the people who keep Florida running. Southern Home Loans is a participating Florida Housing lender, so we can originate your Hometown Heroes loan from pre-approval through closing.

  • 5% of the first mortgage amount, up to $35,000, toward the down payment and closing costs
  • 0% interest, no monthly payment. It's a 30-year deferred second mortgage, due when you sell, refinance, transfer the home or stop living in it.
  • A reduced first mortgage rate on FHA, VA, USDA or conventional 30-year fixed loans

Who qualifies for Hometown Heroes?

  • Eligible full-time Florida jobs: healthcare workers, school staff, first responders, public safety and court employees, childcare workers, active-duty military and reserves, and employed veterans
  • First-time buyer: no ownership of a primary home in the past three years (veterans are exempt)
  • Income at or below 150% of the area median income for your county
  • 640 minimum credit score
  • Homebuyer education course completed before closing
  • Primary residence in Florida

Funding goes fast. The Legislature funds Hometown Heroes one year at a time. The 2025 round was fully committed in six months, and a new $50 million round opened on July 13, 2026. Get pre-approved now so you're ready to reserve funds, and call 352-622-1521 for current availability. If funding runs out, we'll compare Luminate Path and the other Florida Housing programs below.

Florida down payment assistance at a glance

Down payment assistance (DPA) is money that helps cover your down payment and closing costs. In Florida it usually comes as a second mortgage that is forgiven over time, deferred until you sell or refinance, or repaid monthly. Southern Home Loans, a division of Luminate Bank, is a participating Florida Housing lender with its own assistance program, and we help buyers use state and county programs anywhere in Florida.

  • Florida Hometown Heroes: up to $35,000 for first-time buyers in eligible Florida jobs, when state funding is open.
  • Luminate Path: up to 5% of the loan amount from Luminate Bank. No first-time buyer requirement and no income limits.
  • Other Florida Housing programs: FL Assist, FL HLP and HFA PLUS for first-time buyers who meet county income and price limits.
  • County SHIP programs: local help such as the Marion County and Ocala SHIP program when funding is open.

Luminate Path: our own down payment assistance

Luminate Path is Luminate Bank's down payment assistance program, offered in Florida through Southern Home Loans. Eligible buyers get up to 5% of the loan amount to put toward the down payment or closing costs.

  • Up to 5% of the loan amount for down payment or closing costs
  • No first-time buyer requirement. Current and past homeowners can use it.
  • No income limits
  • Two ways to structure it: a 0% interest second loan with no monthly payment that is forgiven after five years, or a 10-year repayable second paired with a lower first mortgage rate
  • 1–2 unit homes anywhere in Florida

Because Luminate Path doesn't depend on state funding, it's still available when Florida's programs run out of money for the year.

Other Florida Housing down payment assistance programs

As a participating lender, we also offer Florida Housing Finance Corporation's other second mortgages that pair with its own 30-year fixed-rate first mortgages: Florida First, HFA Preferred and HFA Advantage. They're for first-time buyers, which generally means you haven't owned your primary home in the past three years, and they have county income and purchase price limits. You can use only one Florida Housing assistance program per loan.

The veterans exception

Honorably discharged veterans don't have to be first-time buyers to use Florida Housing programs. You'll sign a Mortgagor Affidavit and provide your DD214. Veterans should also compare a VA loan, which may make assistance unnecessary.

County and city assistance (SHIP)

Every Florida county receives State Housing Initiatives Partnership (SHIP) funds, and many use part of them for purchase assistance. Amounts, income limits and open dates differ by county. In Marion County and the City of Ocala, the SHIP Purchase Assistance Program can provide substantial help for income-qualified buyers. We also help buyers in Citrus County, Alachua County, Levy County, Hernando County and the rest of Florida check what's open.

Which down payment assistance program is right for you?

  • First-time buyer in an eligible job: start with Hometown Heroes while funding is open.
  • You've owned a home before, or earn above county limits: start with Luminate Path.
  • First-time buyer using a conventional loan: compare HFA PLUS (forgivable) and FL Assist (deferred).
  • Buying in Marion County or Ocala: ask about SHIP alongside your first mortgage.
  • Veteran, or buying in a rural area: compare a VA or USDA loan first.

We run each option side by side, including the rate, monthly payment and cash to close, so you see the real cost of the help before you choose.

Pros and cons of down payment assistance

The upside: you can buy sooner, keep your savings for reserves, repairs and moving costs, and stop paying rent while you save.

The trade-offs: some programs carry a slightly higher first mortgage rate or extra fees, they add a second lien to your home, and many must be repaid if you sell or refinance early. Forgivable programs avoid most of that if you stay in the home long enough.

Frequently asked questions

How much is Florida Hometown Heroes and who qualifies?

Hometown Heroes provides 5% of the first mortgage amount, up to $35,000, as a 0% deferred second mortgage. It's for first-time buyers (veterans exempt) who work full time in eligible Florida jobs such as healthcare, schools, first responders, public safety, childcare and the military, earn up to 150% of area median income and have at least a 640 credit score.

Is Florida Hometown Heroes still available?

Hometown Heroes is funded one year at a time and often runs out within months. A new $50 million round opened on July 13, 2026. Southern Home Loans is a participating Florida Housing lender. Call 352-622-1521 for current availability, and ask about Luminate Path, which doesn't depend on state funding.

What down payment assistance is available in Florida?

Florida buyers can use Florida Hometown Heroes, Luminate Path from Luminate Bank, other Florida Housing programs (FL Assist, FL HLP and HFA PLUS) and county SHIP programs such as Marion County SHIP when funding is open. Southern Home Loans compares the options you qualify for side by side.

Do I have to be a first-time buyer to get down payment assistance?

Not always. Luminate Path has no first-time buyer requirement. Florida Housing programs are for buyers who haven't owned a primary home in the past three years, but honorably discharged veterans are exempt.

Does down payment assistance have to be repaid?

It depends on the program. Some are forgiven over time, such as HFA PLUS and the forgivable Luminate Path option. Others, like FL Assist and Hometown Heroes, are deferred until you sell, refinance or move out. FL HLP is repaid monthly.

What credit score do I need for down payment assistance in Florida?

Florida Housing programs generally require at least a 640 credit score, and Marion County SHIP requires at least 580. Requirements for Luminate Path depend on the loan type. We'll check your options when you apply.

What are the income limits for down payment assistance in Florida?

Florida Housing and SHIP programs set income and purchase price limits by county and household size. Luminate Path has no income limits.

Can I combine down payment assistance programs?

You can use only one Florida Housing assistance program per loan, but some local programs, such as SHIP, may be added as an additional second loan. We'll tell you which combinations work for your purchase.

Can down payment assistance cover closing costs?

Yes. Most programs, including Luminate Path and the Florida Housing programs, can be used for the down payment, closing costs or both.
