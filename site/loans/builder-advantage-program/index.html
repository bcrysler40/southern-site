Sell Homes on Payment, Not Price

Southern Home Loans forward commitments let builders lock in a below-market rate for every qualified buyer. You protect your price and your comps. Your buyers get a payment they can afford.

What is a forward commitment?

You reserve a pool of mortgage money at a fixed, below-market rate. Any qualified buyer who closes with us inside the window gets that rate, no matter where the market goes.

A forward commitment is a marketing budget that works harder than a discount.

How does the Builder Advantage Program work?

  1. Reserve your rate. We size a commitment to your realistic closings over the next build cycle. Pricing depends on the rate you choose and the buyer credit profile.
  2. Market the payment. Put the rate on your signs, listings and model-home materials. We supply co-branded flyers, social ads and payment comparisons.
  3. Close with confidence. Buyers under contract keep the rate through construction. One lender, one process, predictable closings.

Why do builders use it?

More buyers. Fewer price cuts. The Builder Advantage Program helps you:

  • Protect your comps. Keep list prices intact so appraisals in your community hold their value.
  • Qualify more buyers. A lower payment improves debt-to-income, which opens your homes to buyers priced out at market rates.
  • Spend less per sale. Pooled pricing usually costs less per buyer than one-off buydowns or deep price reductions.
  • Move spec inventory. A move-in-ready home with a locked low rate creates real urgency.
  • Reduce fallout. Buyers aren’t exposed to rate swings while their home is under construction.
  • Work with one lender. One point of contact and a direct line to the sales manager, not a call center.
